# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pricing, procurement, category-management and operational users at pharmacy
retailers in Azerbaijan. They work with dense catalog data in Russian and
Azerbaijani and need to decide which prices, matches and assortment gaps require
attention without manually checking several competitor sites.

## Product Purpose

Pharmacy Monitor turns competitor price and assortment collection into trusted,
traceable decisions. Success means users can see which data is fresh, which
cross-site match is reliable, what action is proposed, and what happened after
that action. The product optimizes for verified pricing and assortment decisions,
not dashboard page views.

## Brand Personality

Expert, calm, precise. The interface should create confidence through clear data
provenance, honest status language and familiar product conventions. Copy is
direct and operational rather than promotional.

## Anti-references

- Decorative SaaS dashboards that hide uncertainty behind oversized metrics.
- Consumer shopping interfaces that make operational data feel like a catalog.
- Dense enterprise screens with unexplained abbreviations or ambiguous actions.
- Interfaces that mix languages, hide stale data or present partial runs as valid.
- Novel navigation patterns that make standard workflows harder to predict.

## Design Principles

1. Trust before automation: show freshness, completeness and confidence wherever
   a user may act on a number.
2. One action, one meaning: labels and destinations must agree, especially for
   matching, watchlists and destructive operations.
3. Preserve working context: filters, locale and category scope must survive
   navigation between analysis and correction workflows.
4. Make exceptions visible: empty, stale, partial and untranslated states are
   explicit rather than silently falling back to misleading content.
5. Optimize for decisions: the interface prioritizes what needs review today and
   records the outcome of completed work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
44px touch targets on mobile, meaningful labels, non-color status cues and
reduced-motion preferences. Russian and Azerbaijani content are first-class; a
missing translation must have an explicit, consistent fallback.
